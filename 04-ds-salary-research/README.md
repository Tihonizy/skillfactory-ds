# Data Science Salary Research

Exploratory analysis and statistical testing on data science salaries for 2020-2022 for an HR
agency. The study checks which factors affect a data scientist's salary, whether salaries grow
year over year, how data scientist and data engineer salaries compare in 2022, how salaries
depend on company size, and whether the presence of these roles is related to company size.
Each question is stated as a pair of hypotheses and tested after checking normality and equal
variances.

- Notebook: [01_ds_salary_research.ipynb](01_ds_salary_research.ipynb)
- Tools: pandas, SciPy, statsmodels, category_encoders, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly
