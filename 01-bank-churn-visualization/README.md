# Bank Customer Churn Visualization

A bank wants to design a loyalty campaign and needs to understand how customers who left differ
from loyal ones. The notebook answers ten questions with ten charts on a dataset of bank clients
(demographics, balance, products, activity and churn flag) and writes a conclusion under each chart.

- Notebook: [01_bank_churn_visualization.ipynb](01_bank_churn_visualization.ipynb)
- Tools: pandas, Plotly
- Data: `data/churn.csv` (not tracked)
